01 / About

About me.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst with interests in aerospace, mechanical systems, modeling, and testing. I enjoy working on multidisciplinary engineering problems that combine analysis, simulation, design, and hands-on development.

My experience includes spacecraft hardware development with a CubeSat team, research with the Ocean Renewable Energy Laboratory focused on structural analysis and collapse prevention of ocean thermal energy conversion cold-water pipes, and fault-detection modeling for electromechanical systems using MATLAB, Simulink, and Simscape. Through these projects, I’ve worked with CAD, finite element analysis, dynamic system modeling, signal processing, additive manufacturing, and experimental testing.

I’m especially interested in engineering roles involving aerospace and spacecraft systems, mechanical R&D, systems engineering, and hardware development. I’m currently completing my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at UMass Amherst and expect to graduate in May 2027.
