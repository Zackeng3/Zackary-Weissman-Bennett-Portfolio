01 / About

About me.

I’m a mechanical engineering senior at UMass Amherst, graduating in May 2027. My work spans CubeSat mechanical development, structural finite element analysis, and dynamic modeling of electromechanical systems.

I’m developing a three-axis reaction-wheel test assembly for the UMass CubeSat team and lead motor modeling for a four-person senior design project focused on vibration-based fault detection. My completed research examined ring-stiffened cold-water pipes for ocean thermal energy conversion and was presented at UMERC+METS 2026. I’m seeking opportunities in mechanical R&D, aerospace systems, systems engineering, modeling and simulation, and hardware testing.
